Our Story

Turning local production into everyday essentials.

Since 2000, Ten Day Belt General Trading PLC has been producing food in Ethiopia under the PRIMA name — flour, pasta, macaroni, and biscuits made for everyday life.

What started as a vision to manufacture pasta locally has grown into a operation spanning a 30,000 m² facility, 900+ people, and customers across the country.
