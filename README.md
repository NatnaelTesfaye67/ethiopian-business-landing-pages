# Ethiopian Business Landing Pages

Premium, editorial-style landing pages for Ethiopian businesses — designed and built as speculative/proposal pieces.

## Evertop Sportswear PLC
Bilingual (English/Amharic) landing page for Evertop Sportswear PLC — a garment manufacturer in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Youngone Corporation.

- Dark, industrial B2B editorial aesthetic
- Custom-designed logo treatment
- Amharic/English language toggle
- AOS scroll-driven animations
- Manufacturing process storytelling

## PRIMA — Ten Day Belt General Trading PLC
Cinematic, scroll-driven landing page for PRIMA — a food brand by Ten Day Belt General Trading PLC producing flour, pasta, macaroni and biscuits in Ethiopia.

- Premium FMCG brand campaign aesthetic
- Amharic/English language toggle
- Horizontal-scroll product collection
- Addis Ababa ↔ Dukem geographic storytelling
- Ethiopia distribution map animation
- Interactive macaroni shapes showcase
