# Ethiopian Business Landing Pages

Premium, editorial-style landing pages for established Ethiopian businesses — designed and built as speculative/proposal pieces.

## Projects

| Project | Folder | Description |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Evertop Sportswear PLC | `evertop-sportswear/` | Garment manufacturer, Addis Ababa — Youngone Corporation subsidiary |
| PRIMA — Ten Day Belt General Trading PLC | `prima-ten-day-belt/` | Food manufacturer (flour, pasta, macaroni, biscuits) |
| Enrico Pastry | `enrico-pastry/` | Historic Italian pastry house in Piassa, est. 1954 |
| Peacock Shoe Factory | `peacock-shoe-factory/` | Footwear manufacturer, Addis Ababa |

## Evertop Sportswear PLC
Dark, industrial B2B editorial aesthetic with a custom-designed logo, Amharic/English language toggle, AOS scroll-driven animations and manufacturing process storytelling.

## PRIMA — Ten Day Belt General Trading PLC
Cinematic FMCG brand campaign with a horizontal-scroll product collection, Addis Ababa and Dukem geographic storytelling, an Ethiopia distribution map animation, an interactive macaroni showcase, and an Amharic/English language toggle.

## Enrico Pastry
Heritage-inspired presentation for Pasticceria Enrico, the oldest Italian pastry house in Addis Ababa (Piassa), established 1954.

## Peacock Shoe Factory
Landing page for one of Ethiopia's established footwear manufacturers.

## Notes
- All content is grounded in verified public business information.
- These are speculative design pieces, not commissioned websites.
